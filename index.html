Qingwei Ben

Hi! I’m Qingwei Ben (贲清炜), a second-year Ph.D. student at MMLab, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Dahua Lin. My research focuses on Embodied AI, especially humanoid loco-manipulation and whole-body control. My research goal is to advance full-stack algorithms that bring robots into everyday homes. This summer, I will intern at NVIDIA GEAR Lab.

I am open to conversations around research collaboration, entrepreneurship, and adjacent ideas. Feel free to reach me by email at elgceben@gmail.com, or on WeChat at elgceben.
